Three ways to define "best"

Wins

What the official standings use. Simple, but a 1-point win counts the same as a 30-point blowout, and four weeks in, half the league is tied with someone.

Point differential

Points scored minus points allowed, added up game by game. Close games are close to coin flips, so margin tends to predict future wins better than record does.

Strength-adjusted

Each game becomes one equation: home rating − away rating = margin. With more games than teams there's no exact solution, so the ratings are the least-squares best fit (sum of ratings fixed at 0). Beating a strong team by 10 counts for more than beating a weak one by 10. Expect it to jump around this early in the season.
